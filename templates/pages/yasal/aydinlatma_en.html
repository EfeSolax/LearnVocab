Privacy Notice

your personal data and privacy

This notice is given under Article 10 of the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK). It explains which personal data is processed when you use LearnVocab, why and on what legal basis, who it is shared with and what your rights are. This is a translation; the Turkish text prevails.

1. Data controller

The data controller is {{ veri_sorumlusu }} (learnvocab.com.tr). {% if eposta %}For any question or request: {{ eposta }}.{% endif %}

2. What data do we process?

  • Account data: first and last name, email address, username and password. Your password is stored as a one-way hash; we cannot see it. Optionally, a profile photo and bio.
  • Google sign-in: the name, email address, Google account ID and profile data that Google shares with us. We never see your Google password.
  • Learning data: the words in your list, your Learn & Pass and exercise results, your placement test result, your streak and your settings (interface language, time zone, email and sound preferences).
  • Your writing: texts you write in the Writing section, their feedback and your dialogue answers.
  • Your own texts: text you paste into Reading or that we take from a PDF. The PDF file itself is not stored; only its text is saved to your account.
  • Technical data: session cookies, your IP address (briefly, for security and rate limiting; also in the hosting server's access logs), and words searched for in the dictionary but not found (without recording who searched).

Please do not include sensitive personal data (such as health, religion or political opinions) or other people's personal information in your writing. We do not knowingly process such data.

3. Purposes and legal bases

  • Creating your account, signing you in and providing LearnVocab (dictionary, lists, review, exercises, reading, writing feedback): necessary for entering into and performing a contract (KVKK Art. 5/2-c).
  • Password reset and account emails: performance of a contract (Art. 5/2-c).
  • Reminder and announcement emails: according to the preferences you can switch on and off in Settings; performance of a contract and legitimate interest (Art. 5/2-c, f).
  • Security and preventing abuse and overload (sign-in attempt and request limits): legitimate interest (Art. 5/2-f) and our data security obligations (Art. 5/2-ç, Art. 12).
  • Improving the dictionary (anonymous not-found searches): legitimate interest (Art. 5/2-f).
  • Legal obligations and requests from competent authorities: Art. 5/2-ç.

4. Who is your data shared with?

  • Google (Gemini API): when you submit a text for feedback in the Writing section, the text, the words you chose and the topic are sent to Google's AI service. Your name, email and username are not sent. On this service's free tier, Google may use submitted content to improve its products, and it may be reviewed by humans. We therefore recommend not writing personal information in your texts.
  • Google (Gmail): password reset codes and the emails you opt into are sent through Google's email service, so your email address and the email content are passed to Google.
  • Google sign-in: if you choose it, Google verifies your identity.
  • Hosting: the site and its database run on servers of a hosting company in Turkey.
  • Public authorities: only when required by law.

Google's servers are located outside Turkey, so transfers to Google are transfers abroad and are made in accordance with Article 9 of the KVKK. Your data is never sold or shared for advertising.

5. How do we collect data?

Electronically: from what you enter in the sign-up, sign-in and other forms; from Google when you sign in with Google; and automatically while you use the site (cookies, server logs).

6. How long do we keep it?

  • Account and learning data is kept while your account is open. It is deleted when you delete your account in Settings, and disappears from backups within a few weeks at most.
  • Texts you delete are kept for one day so you can undo, then permanently deleted.
  • Sign-in attempt and rate-limit records are kept for at most 24 hours.
  • Server access logs are kept for as long as the hosting company and the law require.

7. Cookies

We only use cookies that the site needs to work: the session cookie (to keep you signed in), the security cookie (CSRF) and your language choice. Small preferences such as theme and reading font size are stored only in your own browser. We do not use advertising or analytics cookies.

8. Your rights (KVKK Art. 11)

By applying to the data controller, you have the right to:

  • learn whether your personal data is processed and, if so, request information about it,
  • learn the purpose of processing and whether it is used accordingly,
  • know the third parties in Turkey or abroad to whom it is transferred,
  • request correction if it is incomplete or inaccurate,
  • request deletion or destruction under the conditions of KVKK Art. 7,
  • request that corrections and deletions be notified to third parties it was transferred to,
  • object to a result against you arising solely from automated analysis of your data,
  • claim compensation if you suffer damage from unlawful processing.

You can download a copy of your data in the "My data" section of Settings and delete your account in the same place. For other requests, write to {% if eposta %}{{ eposta }}{% else %}us{% endif %}; we answer free of charge within 30 days at the latest. If you are not satisfied with the answer, you can complain to the Turkish Personal Data Protection Board.

9. Changes

If this notice changes, the current version is published on this page and the date above changes.
