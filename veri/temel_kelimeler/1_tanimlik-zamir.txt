a | determiner | A1 | c
  bir (belirsiz tanımlık; sessiz harfle başlayan kelimeden önce) | used before a singular noun that has not been mentioned before, when the next word begins with a consonant sound | I have a brother and a sister. / She is a teacher.
  her, başına (miktar, sıklık) | used to mean "each" or "every" when talking about prices, speeds or how often | I go to the gym three times a week. / Apples are $2 a kilo.
an | determiner | A1 | c
  bir (belirsiz tanımlık; sesli harfle başlayan kelimeden önce) | used instead of "a" when the next word begins with a vowel sound | I ate an apple. / It takes an hour to get there.
the | determiner | A1 | c
  (belirli tanımlık; bilinen, daha önce sözü geçen ya da tek olan şey için) | used before a noun when it is clear which person or thing you mean | Can you close the door? / The sun is very hot today.
  (bir türün tamamı, enstrüman, ırmak, okyanus vb. adlardan önce) | used before some names and to talk about a whole group or type | She plays the piano. / The Nile is the longest river in Africa.
  ne kadar … o kadar | used with two comparative forms to show that two things change together | The more you practice, the better you get. / The sooner, the better.
I | pronoun | A1 | c
  ben | used by the speaker to refer to himself or herself as the subject of a verb | I am a student. / I don't like coffee.
you | pronoun | A1 | c
  sen; siz | used to refer to the person or people you are speaking to | Are you hungry? / You are all welcome.
  insan, kişi (genel olarak herkes) | used to refer to people in general | You can't smoke here. / You never know what will happen.
he | pronoun | A1 | c
  o (erkek) | used to refer to a man, boy or male animal as the subject of a verb | He is my brother. / Where does he live?
she | pronoun | A1 | c
  o (kadın) | used to refer to a woman, girl or female animal as the subject of a verb | She works in a bank. / Is she your sister?
it | pronoun | A1 | c
  o (cansız şey, hayvan, durum) | used to refer to a thing, animal or situation that has already been mentioned | I bought a new phone. It's very fast. / Where's the cat? Is it outside?
  (hava, zaman, mesafe cümlelerinde boş özne) | used as the subject of a verb when talking about the weather, time, distance or a situation | It's raining. / It's ten o'clock. / It's important to sleep well.
we | pronoun | A1 | c
  biz | used by the speaker to refer to himself or herself and one or more other people as the subject of a verb | We are friends. / Shall we go now?
they | pronoun | A1 | c
  onlar | used to refer to two or more people or things as the subject of a verb | They live in Ankara. / Where are my keys? Are they in the car?
  o (cinsiyeti bilinmeyen ya da belirtilmeyen tek kişi) | used to refer to one person when you do not know or do not want to say if it is a man or a woman | Someone called, but they didn't leave a message. / Ask each student if they have a pen.
  onlar, yetkililer, insanlar (belirsiz) | used to refer to people in general or people in authority | They say it will rain tomorrow. / They're building a new hospital.
me | pronoun | A1 | c
  beni, bana | used as the object of a verb or preposition, or after "be", to refer to the speaker | Can you help me? / He gave me a book. / It's me!
him | pronoun | A1 | c
  onu, ona (erkek) | used as the object of a verb or preposition to refer to a man, boy or male animal | I saw him yesterday. / Give the book to him.
her | pronoun | A1 | c
  onu, ona (kadın) | used as the object of a verb or preposition to refer to a woman, girl or female animal | I called her last night. / This present is for her.
us | pronoun | A1 | c
  bizi, bize | used as the object of a verb or preposition to refer to the speaker and another person or other people | Come with us! / They invited us to dinner.
them | pronoun | A1 | c
  onları, onlara | used as the object of a verb or preposition to refer to two or more people or things | I love these shoes. I'll buy them. / Tell them to wait.
  onu, ona (cinsiyeti belirtilmeyen tek kişi) | used to refer to one person when you do not know or do not want to say if it is a man or a woman | If anyone calls, tell them I'm busy. / Every student should bring their book with them.
mine | pronoun | A1 | c

yours | pronoun | A1 | c

his | pronoun | A1 | c

hers | pronoun | A2 | c
  onunki (kadın) | used to refer to something that belongs to a woman, girl or female animal | The red car is hers. / Is this coat yours or hers?
ours | pronoun | A2 | c
  bizimki | used to refer to something that belongs to the speaker and another person or other people | That table is ours. / Their garden is bigger than ours.
theirs | pronoun | A2 | c
  onlarınki | used to refer to something that belongs to two or more people or things | The decision is theirs. / Our kids are older than theirs.
myself | pronoun | A2 | c
  kendimi, kendime (dönüşlü) | used as the object of a verb or preposition when the speaker is both the subject and the object | I hurt myself. / I bought myself a new watch.
  kendim (vurgu için); kendi kendime | used to emphasize "I", or to mean "alone, without help" | I made this cake myself. / I did it all by myself.
yourself | pronoun | A2 | c
  kendini, kendine (dönüşlü) | used as the object of a verb or preposition when the person you are talking to is both the subject and the object | Be careful – don't cut yourself. / Help yourself to some cake.
  kendin (vurgu için); kendi kendine | used to emphasize "you", or to mean "alone, without help" | Did you paint this yourself? / You can't do it all by yourself.
himself | pronoun | A2 | c
  kendini, kendine (erkek, dönüşlü) | used as the object of a verb or preposition when a man or boy is both the subject and the object | He looked at himself in the mirror. / He taught himself to play the guitar.
  kendisi (vurgu için); kendi kendine | used to emphasize a male subject, or to mean "alone, without help" | The president himself came to the opening. / He lives by himself.
herself | pronoun | A2 | c
  kendini, kendine (kadın, dönüşlü) | used as the object of a verb or preposition when a woman or girl is both the subject and the object | She bought herself a new dress. / She hurt herself playing football.
  kendisi (vurgu için); kendi kendine | used to emphasize a female subject, or to mean "alone, without help" | She made the dress herself. / She traveled around Asia by herself.
itself | pronoun | A2 | c
  kendini, kendine (cansız şey, hayvan; dönüşlü) | used as the object of a verb or preposition when a thing or animal is both the subject and the object | The cat is washing itself. / The door opened by itself.
  kendisi (vurgu için) | used to emphasize a thing or animal | The town itself is very small. / The idea itself is good.
ourselves | pronoun | A2 | c
  kendimizi, kendimize (dönüşlü) | used as the object of a verb or preposition when the speaker and others are both the subject and the object | We enjoyed ourselves at the party. / We should be proud of ourselves.
  kendimiz (vurgu için); kendi kendimize | used to emphasize "we", or to mean "alone, without help" | We built the house ourselves. / We did it by ourselves.
yourselves | pronoun | A2 | l
  kendinizi, kendinize (dönüşlü) | used as the object of a verb or preposition when the people you are talking to are both the subject and the object | Enjoy yourselves, kids! / Please help yourselves to drinks.
  kendiniz (vurgu için); kendi kendinize | used to emphasize "you" (plural), or to mean "alone, without help" | Did you make this yourselves? / You'll have to do it by yourselves.
themselves | pronoun | A2 | c
  kendilerini, kendilerine (dönüşlü) | used as the object of a verb or preposition when people or things are both the subject and the object | The children enjoyed themselves. / They introduced themselves.
  kendileri (vurgu için); kendi kendilerine | used to emphasize "they", or to mean "alone, without help" | The students organized the event themselves. / They live by themselves.
this | pronoun | A1 | c
  bu (yakındaki şey ya da kişi) | used to refer to a person or thing that is near you, or to the present situation | This is my sister, Ayşe. / What's this?
that | pronoun | A1 | c
  şu, o (uzaktaki şey ya da kişi; söylenen şey) | used to refer to a person or thing that is not near you, or to something already mentioned | Who's that? / That's a great idea!
  ki, -en, -dığı (ilgi zamiri) | used to introduce a part of a sentence that gives more information about a noun | This is the book that I told you about. / The man that lives next door is a doctor. | which

those | pronoun | A1 | c

  -enler, … olanlar | used to mean "the people who" or "the things which" | Those who finished early can go home. / The best seats are those at the front.
one | pronoun | A2 | c
  (daha önce söz edilen şeyin yerine) olan, -i | used instead of a noun that has already been mentioned | I don't like the blue shirt. I'll take the red one. / Which one do you want?
  insan, kişi (resmî) | used to mean people in general, including the speaker (formal) | One should always be polite. / One never knows.
